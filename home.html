Elevator Pitch

Knowledge Is Power (KIP) is a legal literacy platform that teaches young people the laws that affect their everyday lives through online games.

Starting with Virginia traffic and copyright law, KIP aims to make legal knowledge more accessible, relevant, and easier to retain.

Problem Description

Young people regularly encounter legally binding scenarios—such as driving or creating online content—without any foundational understanding of the laws governing them. Traditional legal texts are dense, dry, and inaccessible, leaving youth vulnerable to costly traffic citations, avoidable legal penalties, and unintentional copyright infringement.

Customer and End User

  • Customer: High schools, community organizations, and parents looking for engaging civic education tools.
  • End User: Teenagers and young adults who need to learn practical legal boundaries in an interactive format.

Solution Statement

KIP bridges this education gap by transforming complex statutes into interactive, gamified learning modules. By starting with highly relevant local topics like Virginia traffic violations and digital copyright regulations, the platform converts boring compliance rules into memorable, gameplay-driven scenarios that reinforce long-term knowledge retention.
