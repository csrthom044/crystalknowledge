1. Introduction

1.1. Societal Problem

Young adults (ages 16–19) often encounter situations involving driving, online activity, and property laws without fully understanding the legal rules and consequences that may apply. As they gain more independence, this lack of legal awareness can lead to poor decisions and serious consequences. Despite this, law-related education remains largely absent from K-12 curricula, leaving young adults underprepared for real world legal situations. Additionally, teen drivers face higher safety risks that are often linked to poor judgement and decision-making. [5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4]

1.2. Needed Solution Characteristics

The game needs to be engaging, accessible, and an interactive educational tool that promotes self-accountability. It should also highlight the actual laws and familiarize users with some legal terms to increase legal literacy.

1.3. Product Introduction

Knowledge Is Power (KIP), a digital platform designed to teach essential laws to young adults, acting as an educational aid for parents and school administrators. Its goal is to help young adults better understand the laws that affect them and make more informed decisions.

2. Knowledge Is Power (KIP) Product Description

2.1. Solution Overview

KIP utilizes a focused, game-based interface paired with real-world video assets to educate young adults on practically-applicable legal boundaries.

2.2. Goals and Objectives

To assess, improve, and reinforce legal knowledge among tech-savvy youth in 2026 through low-stakes, engaging interactivity. This interface aims to increase recognition of legal situations, improve decision making skills, and strengthen long-term retention of legal concepts.
