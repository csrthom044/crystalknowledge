1. Introduction

Young people ages 16–19 often encounter laws involving driving, digital conduct, work, school, ownership, contracts, and safety without fully understanding their inner workings and the consequences that may apply [5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4]. As they gain more independence into adulthood, this lack of legal awareness can lead to poor decisions and serious consequences. Despite this, law-related education remains largely absent from K-12 curricula, leaving young adults underprepared for real world legal situations, while teen drivers face higher safety risks linked to poor judgment. The needed solution platform must be an engaging, accessible educational tool that promotes responsible decision making, references actual legislative sources, and familiarizes users with legal vocabulary to maximize legal literacy. Knowledge Is Power (KIP) is a digital platform designed to teach essential laws to young adults, acting as an educational aid for parents and school administrators to help youth make informed decisions. The approach to demonstrating KIP's core educational characteristics and validation metrics will be achieved through targeted web prototyping architectures.

2. Knowledge Is Power (KIP) Product Description

Knowledge Is Power (KIP) utilizes a focused, game-based interface which relies on real-world examples, curated media, and official resources to educate young adults on practically-applicable legal boundaries. The platform works to assess, improve, and reinforce legal knowledge among tech-savvy youth in 2026 through low-stakes, engaging interactivity. This interface aims to increase real-world recognition of legal situations, improve critical decision-making skills, and strengthen long-term retention of legal concepts.

2.1. Key Product Features and Capabilities

  • Interactive Gamification: Employs succinct, timed, quirky games to assess current legal knowledge.
  • "Mythbuster" Game: Short-form game quizzing users on common legal misconceptions with true, false, or context-dependent options.
  • "Branching Path" Game: Multiple-choice, long-form game encouraging users to interact with scenarios and documents before confirming paths.

2.2. Major Components (Hardware/Software)

  • Hardware Requirements: Operations supported on standard consumer smartphones, tablets, and computers.
  • System Architecture: Structured precisely according to the CS 410 Major Functional Component Diagram (MFCD).
  • Software to be Developed: Web-based application interface, interactive game engines, and video widget modules.

3. Identification of Case Study

  • Primary Target Audience: Young adults aged 16–19 navigating high-risk legal areas where legal consequences become impactful but formal education is limited.
  • Secondary Stakeholders: Parents, teachers, and high school administrators using KIP as a supplemental educational tool to reinforce legal awareness outside the classroom.
  • Future Users: Juvenile diversion programs, community youth groups, and driving schools using KIP assets in structured settings.
